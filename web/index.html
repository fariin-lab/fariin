Kulan

Private messaging. No phone number needed.

Kulan is a messenger for iPhone. You sign up with just a name and a username, find friends by username or QR code, and chat knowing your messages are end-to-end encrypted. It also has voice and video calls, voice messages, and stories that disappear after 24 hours.

Kulan is a Somali word for a gathering, a place where people meet.
