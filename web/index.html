What you get

A full messenger, built around privacy from the first screen.

No phone number

Create an account with just a name and a username. Find friends by username or by scanning their QR code in person.

End-to-end encrypted

Texts, photos, videos, voice messages and files are encrypted on your device. Only you and the recipient can read them.

Voice and video calls

Free 1-to-1 calls over the internet, encrypted in transit.

Stories

Photo and video stories that disappear after 24 hours. You choose who sees them, and you can see who viewed them.

Voice messages

Hold to record, with waveforms and playback speed control.

Locked down

App Lock with Face ID, screen security, disappearing messages, and block and report built in. Delete your account any time.

kulan

A Somali word for a gathering, a place where people meet. That is what the app is meant to be: a private place to talk with the people who matter to you.
