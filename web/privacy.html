Kulan Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Kulan is a messaging app. This policy explains what information Kulan handles and how. By using Kulan you agree to this policy.

Information we handle

  • Your profile: the display name, username (handle), and optional photo and bio you choose. You do not need a phone number or an email address to use Kulan.
  • Messages & media: the texts, photos, videos, voice messages, files, and locations you share in chats, so they can be delivered to the people you chat with.
  • Stories: the photos and videos you post as stories, with their captions.
  • An account identifier: an anonymous sign-in ID used to keep you signed in.
  • Notification token: a device token so we can deliver push notifications for new messages and calls.

Kulan has no ads and no analytics or tracking software. We collect nothing beyond what the app needs to work.

How your data is stored

Kulan uses Google Firebase (Firestore, Storage, Authentication, Cloud Messaging) to store and deliver your data. All data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections.

Encryption

Everything you send inside a chat (texts, photos, videos, voice messages, files, and shared locations) is end-to-end encrypted once both people have opened Kulan. Only the sender and the recipient can read it. Kulan cannot, and neither can the server.

Voice and video calls are encrypted in transit.

Stories and profile information are not end-to-end encrypted. Stories are visible only to the audience you choose inside the app, and they are deleted automatically after 24 hours.

Safety and reporting

Kulan has zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive behavior. You can block any user and report any message or user from inside the app. Reports are reviewed and acted on within 24 hours, which may include removing content and banning offending users.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not show ads.
  • We do not share your messages with third parties.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account and its associated data at any time from inside the app: Settings → Account → Delete Account. This permanently removes your profile and signs you out. You may also email us at the address below.

Children

Kulan is intended for users aged 13 and older.
